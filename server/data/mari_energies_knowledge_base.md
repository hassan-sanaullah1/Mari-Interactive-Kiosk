## Mari Energies Limited: company overview

Mari Energies Limited (formerly Mari Petroleum Company Limited) is a Pakistani integrated energy company whose core business is upstream oil and gas exploration and production. Its head office is in Islamabad.

**Principal Business Activities**

Mari Energies Limited's core business is upstream oil and gas exploration and production. The company also has a services division offering gravity & magnetic services, seismic acquisition & processing, mud logging and drilling services for inhouse needs as well as for other E&P companies. The company has also expanded into minerals mining, focusing on copper, gold and rare earth elements (REEs), and technology, including data centers. In addition, through GHG Emissions Mitigation Limited (GEM), the company is investing in emissions reduction and gas recovery initiatives, further supporting resource growth, innovation and sustainability.

### Shareholding Structure & Group Relationships

MariEnergies is a public limited company operating in the private sector, with management control vested in Fauji Foundation.

The Company is listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, with a market capitalization of around Rs. 810 billion as on June 30, 2026.

Its major shareholders include:

· Fauji Foundation (40%),

· Government of Pakistan (20%),

· Oil and Gas Development Company Ltd (20%), and

· General Public (20%)

All Fauji Foundation group companies are associated companies of MariEnergies. The Company wholly owns Mari Minerals (Pvt) Ltd and Mari Technologies Ltd. It also holds 51% shareholding in GHG Emissions Mitigation Ltd and 25% equity stake in Pakistan International Oil Ltd (PIOL), Abu Dhabi. Through MariTechnologies, the Company holds majority interest (60%) in Sky47 Ltd, while MariMinerals holds interest in several project companies undertaking mineral exploration and core handling and testing services.

**History and milestones**

*Strength in Legacy, Leadership in Innovation*

The history of Mari Energies is deeply rooted in the discovery of Mari Gas Field – the mother field of our company and one of the largest indigenous natural gas reserves in Pakistan. Mari Gas Field was first discovered in 1954 under the Pakistan Stanvac Petroleum Project (PSPP), a landmark venture between the Government of Pakistan and Esso Eastern Incorporated, USA with 49% and 51% ownership interests respectively.

**1957 - Mari Field Discovered**

Mari Field discovered by Esso Eastern with original gas in place estimated at 2.38 TCF, later enhanced to 10.751 TCF.

**1967 - Commencement of Gas Production**

Production from the Mari field started with the supply of gas to Esso's newly established urea plant (now Engro) in the vicinity of the field.

**1983 - Acquisition of Esso's Interest**

Fauji Foundation, OGDCL & GoP acquired Esso's entire operations, including Mari Field.

### Vision 2030: Roadmap to excellence in energy transformation

Recognizing that true energy resilience extends beyond hydrocarbons, MariEnergies began a purposeful expansion into high potential, future-facing sectors. ### What are the business units of Mari Energies?

- Mari Services: the in-house, integrated exploration and production services arm. It offers seismic acquisition and processing, mud logging, gravity and magnetic surveys and drilling services across Pakistan's oil and gas sector, both for Mari Energies' own operations and for other E&P companies. Website: mariservices.com.pk.
- Mari Minerals (Pvt.) Limited: a wholly owned subsidiary established in 2023 as part of the diversification strategy, focused on copper, gold and rare earth elements (REE) mining.
- Mari Technologies Limited and Sky47: Mari Technologies is a wholly owned subsidiary leading the expansion into the technology domain through large-scale data centers. Sky47 Limited is 60% owned through Mari Technologies, with Paramount Ventures at 30% and Capital Smart Technologies at 10%. Website: sky47.com.pk.
- GEM Energy (GHG Emissions Mitigation Limited): a joint venture in which Mari Energies holds 51% with Ghani Chemical Industries Limited, for methane mitigation, LNG and food-grade carbon dioxide. The earlier knowledge base listed it as a fourth vertical.

### What is the GEM Energy project and how is it financed?

GEM Energy (GHG Emissions Mitigation Limited) is a joint venture between Mari Energies (51% equity stake) and Ghani Chemical Industries Limited. The project is located near the Sachal Gas Processing Complex (SGPC) at Mari Field, Daharki, Sindh. It is designed to mitigate methane emissions by recovering hydrocarbons from exhaust and flare gas to produce liquefied natural gas (LNG) as well as industrial-grade and food-grade carbon dioxide (CO2).

At an Extraordinary General Meeting on 22 May 2026, Mari Energies shareholders approved a financial and strategic backing package for GEM. It has two parts. First, a direct equity injection of up to PKR 2.448 billion, through subscription to 244.8 million right shares at PKR 10 face value. Second, a Sponsor Support Agreement backing GEM's project financing, including a Letter of Credit facility of up to USD 38 million and a funded debt facility of up to PKR 14.4 billion, which together make combined debt facilities of roughly PKR 25 billion. Mari Energies' sponsor support is proportional to its shareholding and covers potential cost overruns, base equity commitments and Debt Service Reserve Account (DSRA) requirements.

### Exploration Overview

MariEnergies has established itself as a leading energy explorer with a broad and diversified upstream portfolio, underpinned by disciplined execution and strategic expansion across onshore and offshore hydrocarbon frontiers. The company holds one of the largest exploration and development portfolios in the region, comprising 72 exploration licenses and multiple development and production leases across key basins.

A significant recent milestone in MariEnergies' growth has been its success in the competitive offshore licensing round. The company secured provisional awards for 23 new offshore exploration blocks, including operatorship of 18 blocks, many with 100 percent working interest, across the Indus and Makran basins. On the onshore front, MariEnergies continues to advance frontier exploration and play development. The portfolio now reflects a comprehensive footprint across conventional and frontier basins, both domestically and through select international interests, demonstrating a balanced approach between near-term value creation and long-term exploration upside.

Through its expansive portfolio, leadership in offshore operatorship, and self-sufficient technical capabilities, MariEnergies is uniquely positioned to deliver significant resource additions, drive exploration success across multiple basins, and generate sustainable long-term value for stakeholders. Its integrated approach ensures the company can operate effectively in both established and frontier regions, while actively supporting Pakistan's energy security, economic growth, and domestic energy supply, reinforcing its commitment to contributing positively to the country and the wider energy sector.

### What are Mari Energies' key field development highlights? (Shewa, Spinwam, Waziristan, Sujawal, Bhitai-6)

Mari Energies' key field development highlights are the Shewa and Spinwam developments in the Waziristan Block, the Sujawal facility expansion, the Bhitai-6 smart well and the HRL Pressure Enhancement Facilities project at Mari Field.

- Shewa, Waziristan Block: production commenced on March 23, 2025. The Early Production Facility was commissioned with capacity of up to 100 MMSCFD of gas, and SNGPL completed a 230 km, 18-inch pipeline from Shewa to Daud Khel. The first condensate tanker went to Attock Refinery Limited on March 29, 2025. The two Shewa wells can contribute up to 70 MMSCFD of gas and 700 barrels per day of condensate. The Declaration of Commerciality has been submitted and the Full Field Development Plan is underway. Delivered with joint venture partners OGDCL and Orient Petroleum.
- Spinwam-1, Waziristan Block: multiple productive formations (Samanasuk, Kawagarh, Hangu, Lockhart). Production was inaugurated at a ceremony reported on April 3, 2026, with initial gas supply to SNGPL of about 40 MMSCFD and 200 barrels per day of condensate. Total production from the Waziristan Block (Shewa and Spinwam together) is around 100 MMSCFD of gas and 800 barrels per day of condensate. A new processing facility is planned to handle gas from both Shewa and Spinwam.
- Sujawal facility expansion: the Soho-1 discovery flowed 36 MMscfd from the Lower Goru (Massive Sand) Formation. Jhim East X-1 began gas injection at the Sujawal Facility in December 2024 (about 10 MMSCFD); first gas from Pateji X-1 began in April 2025 (about 8 MMSCFD) through a pipeline of about 40 km, with first gas delivered to SSGC on April 1, 2025. A new facility layout adds 30 MMSCFD of capacity.
- Bhitai-6 well, Mari D&PL: the country's first SMART well, with advanced downhole instrumentation and remotely operated flow control valves, completed by April 2025.
- HRL Pressure Enhancement Facilities (PEF) project at Mari Field: implemented with fertilizer customers to manage declining reservoir pressure and sustain the HRL production plateau.
- Mari Field Ghazij reservoir: a landmark discovery, with 11 wells already on production under the Phase-I development plan.

## Mari Technologies and Sky47

Sky47 Limited is the data center, sovereign cloud and AI infrastructure company of the Mari Energies group. Mari Technologies Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Mari Energies, holds 60% of Sky47. Sky47's first data center, Karakoram-01 in Islamabad, was inaugurated on July 24, 2026.

### What is Sky47?

Sky47 Limited is a Mari Energies group company that builds and runs Tier III/IV data centers, sovereign cloud services and AI infrastructure in Pakistan. It was incorporated on November 28, 2024 by Mari Technologies Limited. Its mandate is to develop Tier III/IV-certified data center infrastructure and build out the wider digital ecosystem across data centers, cloud platforms and AI-enabled services. Its flagship achievement so far is the completion and inauguration of the Islamabad data center, Karakoram-01.

### Who owns Sky47?

Mari Technologies Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Mari Energies, holds 60% of Sky47 Limited. Paramount Ventures (Pvt.) Limited holds 30% and Capital Smart Technologies holds the remaining 10%.

### What is Mari Technologies?

Mari Technologies Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Mari Energies. It leads the company's expansion into the technology domain through the establishment of large-scale data centers, and holds Mari Energies' 60% stake in Sky47. Mari Energies presents this technology push, together with Mari Minerals, as part of its Vision 2030 strategy of diversifying beyond oil and gas.

### What is Karakoram-01?

Karakoram-01 is Sky47's data center campus in Islamabad. It was officially inaugurated on July 24, 2026 by the Prime Minister of Pakistan, Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif, in a ceremony attended by the Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, the Federal Minister for IT and Telecommunication Ms. Shaza Fatima Khawaja, and Fauji Group Chairman Lt Gen Anwar Ali Hyder (Retd). Sky47's CEO is Hassan Abbas.

Karakoram-01 is described as Pakistan's first purpose-built, AI-ready data center campus and the first Tier III data center in the country. It brings together enterprise-grade data center infrastructure, sovereign cloud services, high-performance AI computing, cybersecurity and managed technology services in one ecosystem, serving government, financial, telecommunications, healthcare, energy and technology clients.

### What are the facts and figures of Karakoram-01?

- Total power capacity: 8.5 MW, with over 1,000 planned racks.
- It hosts the country's largest purpose-built Cloud and AI farm.
- Built and energized within 12 months of groundbreaking, ahead of the 18-month international benchmark for projects of this scale.
- Earned the Uptime Institute's Tier-III Certification of Design Documents; further certifications are progressing in phases.
- Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of 1.4 and zero Water Usage Effectiveness: air-cooled chillers, no water used for cooling.
- Delivered with global technology partner ZTE Corporation: air-cooled chiller plant, prefabricated power modules and intelligent busbars.
- Pakistan's first installation of the Cummins Centum Force diesel generator system with integrated bulk fuel storage.
- Supports high-density workloads of 50 kW and above per rack, with future-ready direct-to-chip liquid cooling for AI and GPU applications.
- Offers Pakistan's first dual-stack sovereign cloud, a containerized and modular design, carrier neutrality through multiple fiber providers, layered cybersecurity and a dedicated disaster-recovery site.
- Live from day one: locally deployed large language models customized on Pakistan-based data, a first step toward sovereign AI; AI and cloud platforms commissioned; first paying customers onboarded.

### How much power does the Sky47 data center have?

The Karakoram-01 data center in Islamabad has a total power capacity of 8.5 MW and over 1,000 planned racks. It supports 50 kW and above per rack, with a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of 1.4 and zero Water Usage Effectiveness. Sky47 has a 50 MW expansion roadmap across Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore.

### What is Sky47's next data center?

Sky47 is developing its second facility, Indus-1, at the Foundation Renewable Technology Zone in Port Qasim, Karachi. Pre-construction activities are underway and overall completion is targeted for 2027. The Karachi facility is envisioned as Pakistan's first Tier-IV data center, advanced through phased design and preparatory work, with execution timelines to be finalized after detailed planning. Sky47 is pursuing a growing footprint across Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore, underpinned by a 50 MW expansion roadmap. To fill capacity it is engaging hyperscalers, telecom operators and public and private sector stakeholders, and is pursuing enhanced Cloud Service Provider accreditation to host public-sector workloads.

### What services does Sky47 offer?

Sky47 offers four groups of services: Sky47 Cloud (sovereign cloud), Sky47 Manage and Sky47 Secure (managed and security services), Sky47 AI (GPU as a Service and AI solutions) and Sky47 Space (carrier-neutral colocation from the Tier III/IV facilities). Details of each follow.

### Sky47 Cloud: sovereign cloud services

- Sky47 Cloud is Sky47's sovereign cloud platform, commercially live since 15 January 2026, built to give Pakistani enterprises a secure, scalable cloud for digital transformation without offshore dependency.
- Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS): virtual machines, auto-scaling and high-performance compute.
- Storage as a Service: high-performance block storage and object storage.
- Platform as a Service (PaaS): Kubernetes container service, managed databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL), distributed cache (Redis), a big data platform, and application operations with log tracing.
- Network as a Service: load balancer, IPSEC VPN, Direct Connect dedicated connectivity, elastic IP and NAT gateway.
- Business continuity: Backup as a Service, Disaster Recovery as a Service and database replication.
- Built on Huawei Cloud Stack (HCS), with pay-as-you-go and allocation-based pricing and a 99.95% uptime SLA.
- Complies with Pakistan's Cloud-First Policy and data residency mandates, for data privacy, regulatory alignment, low latency and predictable pricing.

### Sky47 managed and security services

- Sky47 Manage provides end-to-end operational management of cloud infrastructure, databases and containerized workloads, backed by SLAs and dedicated 24/7 engineering support.
- Managed Infrastructure: lifecycle management, 24/7 monitoring, patching, auto-scaling, and security group and access control optimization.
- Managed Database: architecture design and deployment for MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server and MongoDB, with automated backup, replication, failover, query tuning and RPO/RTO compliance.
- Managed Kubernetes: cluster provisioning, policy-based access control, CI/CD pipeline integration, high-availability configuration and day-2 operations support.
- Managed NOC/SOC: performance tuning, capacity forecasting, unified dashboards, compliance-ready reporting and continuous security posture improvement.
- Sky47 Secure delivers the group's cybersecurity services, and Sky47 Space delivers carrier-neutral colocation from the same Tier III/IV facilities.

### Sky47 AI: GPU as a Service and AI solutions

- Sky47 AI has been commercially live since 15 January 2026 and operates Pakistan's largest Cloud AI Farm.
- GPU as a Service and AI infrastructure: Huawei Ascend NPU and NVIDIA-compatible GPU clusters, up to 50 kW per rack density, liquid-cooled for AI/ML training and inference.
- AI as a Service marketplace: pre-built models, partner APIs and ready-to-deploy applications including large language models, RAG, Vision AI, Speech AI, OCR and Pangu Brain RAG.
- Pay-as-you-grow, consumption-based billing for GPU and AI services.
- Industry solutions span enterprise (customer support RAG, call center QA, voice agents, document AI), finance (fraud detection, financial crime analysis, risk modeling), healthcare (AI radiology, virtual medical scribe, AI drug discovery), industrial and mobility (predictive maintenance, construction monitoring, driver safety) and government (national education tutor, public safety, border and port security AI).
- Custom AI development covers purpose-built models, tailored ML pipelines and enterprise AI integration, deployed on data-sovereign, low-latency infrastructure.

### What is the Sky47 and Huawei partnership?

- Sky47 signed its partnership with Huawei in September 2025 at the 2nd Pakistan-China B2B Conference in China.
- Scope: collaboration to strengthen Pakistan's digital infrastructure by enabling secure cloud services, scalable AI deployments, and compliance with the Cloud-First Policy, ensuring both data sovereignty and data residency.
- Key milestone: Huawei data center containers were energized at the Sky47 Islamabad campus in December 2025, and the Cloud AI Farm was developed in collaboration with Huawei and DWP (Digital World Pakistan).
- Huawei technology underpins Sky47's core platforms: Huawei Cloud Stack powers Sky47 Cloud, and Huawei Ascend NPUs power the GPU as a Service and AI Farm compute.
- Facility infrastructure for Karakoram-01 (cooling, power modules, busbars) was delivered with ZTE Corporation; Huawei supplies the cloud and AI platform layer.

- Mari Technologies Limited: wholly owned subsidiary leading the expansion into data centers; holds 60% of Sky47 Limited.

### Smart Completion in Bhitai-6 Well (Mari D&PL)

Country's first ever SMART well has been successfully completed by April 2025 which marks a historic milestone. This achievement underscores our unwavering commitment to innovation and operational excellence in the upstream energy sector. The deployment of smart well completion technology represents a transformative advancement in well architecture and reservoir management. Unlike conventional completions, smart completions are equipped with advanced downhole instrumentation and remotely operated flow control valves, allowing real-time monitoring and dynamic adjustment of zonal flow, without requiring well intervention.

Key benefits of this technology include: • Optimized production performance through selective zonal control • Proactive management of water and gas breakthrough, extending reservoir life • Enhanced hydrocarbon recovery, leading to greater field productivity • Reduction in operational costs and deferred production losses • Minimized environmental footprint by reducing the need for additional wells

In multi layer-reservoir settings, smart completions empower to manage each zone independently within a single wellbore, significantly improving the economic and technical feasibility of complex field developments.

At MariEnergies, we are committed to pushing the boundaries of innovation and establishing new industry benchmarks. The successful deployment of Pakistan's first SMART well is not just a technological achievement, but a testament to our leadership in shaping the future of energy in the country.

## Unlocking Waziristan's Potential

From Shewa to Spinwam, MariEnergies is proving that bold exploration, even in the toughest terrains, can yield game-changing results. Our journey in the Waziristan Block has only just begun.

### Shewa

The successful commissioning of Shewa Early Production Facility (EPF) and the commencement of production from Shewa-1 and Shewa-2 wells in the Waziristan Block mark a major milestone in MariEnergies' journey to unlock Pakistan's frontier basins and strengthen our national energy security. This is not only a technical achievement but a endorsement of our vision to unlock hydrocarbon potential in Pakistan's most challenging frontier basins. Following the significant discovery at Shewa-1 well and subsequent appraisal through Shewa-2, the company fast-tracked the installation of an Early Production Facility (EPF) to enable early monetization. The facility was successfully commissioned with a processing and export capacity of up to 100 MMSCFD of gas. Overcoming complex terrain & volatile security conditions, SNGPL completed a 230 km, 18-inch transmission pipeline from Shewa to Daud Khel, connecting the field to the national grid.

After system integration and safety review, gas and condensate production commenced on March 23, 2025. A performance test with the O&M contractor, as required under the Gas Processing Contract, was successfully completed on March 28, followed by the dispatch of the first condensate tanker to Attock Refinery Limited on March 29, 2025. After ramp up and stabilization of plant operations, the two Shewa wells are able to contribute up to 70 MMSCFD of gas and 700 BPD of condensate, generating substantial revenue for the Joint Venture. The Declaration of Commerciality (DoC) for the Shewa discovery has been submitted to the Regulator, while work on the Full Field Development Plan (FDP) is underway. The success of this project is a testament to the strong partnership with OGDCL and Orient Petroleum, whose steadfast support has been instrumental throughout the journey.

### Spinwam-1 Discovery: Powering Pakistan's Next Energy Frontier

The Waziristan Block continued to yield remarkable results with the discovery of multiple productive reservoirs at the Spinwam-1 exploration well, significantly enhancing MariEnergies' organic growth outlook. Outstanding flow rates were recorded from the Samanasuk, Kawagarh, Hangu, and Lockhart formations, confirming the presence of a rich and multi-layered hydrocarbon system. The combined estimated in-place volume across these formations is a substantial resource base that strengthens the strategic value of the block. The Spinwam-1 well alone, if fully opened has the potential to place it amongst Pakistan's highestproducing individual wells. In response to this high-impact discovery, MariEnergies is advancing plans to install a new processing facility capable of handling gas from both Shewa and Spinwam wells. Once operational, this would increase the total Waziristan Block production capacity from current levels. Moreover, additional exploration leads within the block may hold further upside potential, possibly raising the block's output further. This momentum, combined with adjacent blocks, presents an opportunity to develop a major new hydrocarbon hub in this region. To capitalize on this potential, MariEnergies is actively engaging with OGDCL, SNGPL, and Alhaj for infrastructure collaboration and coordinated field development. However, the pace of expansion remains contingent upon improvements in the security environment and the timely availability of critical pipeline infrastructure. With its bold exploration strategy, early production successes, and game-changing discoveries like Spinwam-1, MariEnergies is positioning the Waziristan Block and surrounding licenses as a transformative asset – one that could redefine Pakistan's domestic gas landscape and deliver long-term energy resilience.

### Mari Field (Operated field)

- Location: Daharki, District Ghotki, Sindh
- Producing Reservoirs: HRL, Goru-B, PKL, SML, SUL, Ghazij
- Hydrocarbon Type: Gas and Condensate
- Producing wells: 168
- Gross Sales Production Capacity: 880 MMscfd
- Working Interest: 100%
- Operatorship: Operated

Mari Field, MariEnergies' flagship asset, located in Ghotki District, Sindh, is Pakistan's largest gas-producing field and a cornerstone of the Company's portfolio. Discovered in 1957, the field began production in 1967 and has since been developed across multiple reservoirs, including Habib Rahi Limestone (HRL), Pirkoh Limestone (PKL), Sui Upper Lime (SUL), Sui Main Lime (SML), Lower Goru B, and the recently discovered Ghazij formation.

Supported by an extensive network of 530 kilometers of pipelines, Mari Field plays a pivotal role in national food security by supplying natural gas to fertilizer plants, meeting more than 90% of the Country's urea requirements. Since its inception, it has maintained reliable production, ensuring a consistent gas supply for both feedstock and fuel.

HRL, the backbone of Mari Field, was the first reservoir developed in Mari Field and has played a central role in Pakistan's energy supply. Production began in 1967 with six wells, followed by additional drilling in multiple development phases to increase production.

As of July 2025, 127 wells have been drilled in HRL, with 121 currently in production. In 2022, the field achieved a major milestone with its first-ever pilot horizontal well, followed by four additional horizontal wells, marking an important milestone in its field development.

Continuous improvement through projects such as debottlenecking, compression, and production optimization has helped maintain gas production and effective reservoir management. With ongoing enhancements planned, HRL remains a key contributor to Mari Field's production and continues to support Pakistan's energy security.

SML-SUL Fields are dry gas reservoirs within Mari Field, consisting of Bhitai, Shahbaz, Shaheen, Hilal, SML, and Iqbal fields, each distinguished by different fault blocks and gas-water contacts.

Discovered through SML-1 in 2005, followed by Bhitai-1discovery in 2007 and subsequent 3D seismic-driven discoveries, the fields continue to contribute to Mari Field's production. As of July 2025, 12 wells are on production.

Recent developments include Pakistan's first smart completion well in 2024, along with planned infill wells to sustain plateau production. Future initiatives focus on booster compression, rigless interventions, and reservoir optimization to enhance recovery and ensure consistent gas supply.

Pirkoh Limestone (PKL) reservoir was discovered in 2005 with the SML-1 well. Since discovery, seven more wells have been drilled in PKL. Currently, the gas from this reservoir is primarily being used as fuel.

Goru-B reservoir, also known as Mari Deep, is one of the key producing zones within Mari D&PL. It was discovered in 1997 through the drilling of the Mari Deep-1 exploratory well, which confirmed the presence of hydrocarbons in the Lower Goru-B sandstone formation. Regular production from the field commenced in 2009.

Goru-B is a sandstone reservoir characterized by multiple vertically stacked sand bodies interbedded with thin shale layers. The reservoir primarily contains lean gas with a relatively high CO2 content. As of July 2025, 22 wells have been drilled in the reservoir, most of which are active gas producers. Continuous monitoring and optimization initiatives have been undertaken to manage production performance and water handling across the field.

Integrated with the Sachal Gas Processing Complex, Goru-B plays an important role in supporting Mari Field's long-term production and energy contribution to the Country.

The Ghazij Reservoir, a recent landmark discovery at Mari Field, perfectly illustrates how nature conceals its treasures. The reservoir had been penetrated by more than 30 wells with no signs of hydrocarbons. MariEnergies re-evaluated all horizons in an effort to revitalize the field and planned an exploratory well within the Ghazij Member which is mainly composed of shale with very thin limestone stringers, having thickness ranging from 0.75 to 2.0 m. In 2022, the pilot well Mari Ghazij-1 proved the reservoir potential of these limestone layers, and subsequent appraisal and development wells confirmed reservoir extent. With a fast-paced development, 11 wells have already been put on production, with a few more wells in drilling and tie-in stages. The development is being carried out under Phase-I development plan, while Phase-II is being planned to further increase the field's production potential. Ghazij contributes to Mari Field's overall output, supporting Pakistan's energy supply, and highlights the complex geology of the reservoir that requires meticulous planning and management.

## Technology is changing the way we approach safety, but the purpose remains the same - protecting people.

October 21, 2026

MariEnergies is pleased to share the successful launch of the AI-HSE Compliance & Monitoring System at Sachal Gas Processing Complex within Mari field Daharki, an important step towards bringing together the experience of traditional HSE practices with the possibilities of AI-enabled monitoring. Over the period, HSE performance has been strengthened through field vigilance, supervision, inspections, coaching and timely intervention. ## Historic Milestone for Pakistan's Energy Sector

January 19, 2026

Mari Energies Pakistan is proud to announce the successful installation of the first permanent fiber‐optics monitoring system in a gas well in Pakistan. A breakthrough achievement that marks a new era of well surveillance and reservoir insight. This pioneering installation leverages advanced DTS (Distributed Temperature Sensing) and DAS (Distributed Acoustic Sensing) technologies, enabled through Baker Hughes SureVIEWTM CoreBright fiber‐optic systems. DTS & DAS deliver continuous temperature and acoustic profiles along the entire wellbore length, enabling real‐time detection of zonal contribution, stimulation effectiveness, crossflow, and integrity issues. This high‐resolution distributed data helps in optimizing injection/ production strategies. With this milestone, MariEnergies reinforces its commitment to deploying cutting-edge technologies that enhance reservoir understanding, improve efficiency, and support the country's long‐term energy sustainability. A huge thank you to our exceptional technical teams for their dedication in the design, planning and flawless execution of this project.
