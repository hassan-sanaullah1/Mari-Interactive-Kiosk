## 1. Company Overview
### 1.1 What is Sky47?
Sky47 Limited is Pakistan's leading sovereign digital infrastructure provider. The company develops state-of-the-art, Tier III/IV certified, purpose-built data centers across Pakistan focused on cloud hosting, colocation, AI infrastructure, and quantum and cloud-native infrastructure. Sky47 is positioned as the country's largest cloud and AI infrastructure platform currently in operation.

Sky47 is aligned with Pakistan's Cloud-First Policy and ensures data privacy, security, and localization through its advanced infrastructure. The company's tagline is **"Secure. Scalable. Sovereign."** and its mission statement is **"Powering Pakistan's Digital Future."**

### 1.2 Legal Entity and Incorporation
- **Full Legal Name:** Sky47 Limited

- **Regulatory Licensing:** Awarded the STZA (Special Technology Zones Authority) Zone Enterprise License in July 2025

### 1.3 Ownership Structure

**Sky47 Shareholding:**
• Mari Energies Limited and Mari Technologies Limited together hold a 60% ownership stake in Sky47 Limited, making them the majority shareholder with management control.
• Paramount Ventures holds a 30% minority ownership stake in Sky47 Limited.

**Ownership Chain Clarification:**
• Mari Energies Limited (PSX-listed; formerly Mari Petroleum Company Limited) is the ultimate parent company of Sky47 through a two-tier structure:
  - Mari Energies owns 100% of Mari Technologies Limited

**Commercial Status:** Launching July 21, 2026 (alongside the Islamabad Data Center inauguration).  

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Rack Configurations:** 4kW to 50kW per rack (standard, high-density, and ultra-high-density AI/ML optimized)
- **Scalability:** Single racks to private cages and dedicated enterprise suites
- **Power & Cooling:** Redundant N+1 power architecture, 8 MW base load per campus (scalable to 16 MW), advanced liquid cooling for high-density AI workloads, hot/cold aisle containment
- **Connectivity:** True carrier-neutral architecture with multiple ISPs (Wateen, Jazz, and others), diverse routing, and low-latency local exchange access
- **Security & Compliance:** 24/7 biometric/multi-factor access, CCTV surveillance, mantraps, ISO/PCI-DSS aligned infrastructure

**SLA & Guarantee:** 99.98% uptime guarantee for colocation services  
**Value:** Mission-critical, carrier-neutral facility designed for AI, cloud-native, and enterprise workloads with full data sovereignty.
